Research integrity and release gates

Never claim: model validation through FY2026; clean untouched FY2014/FY2024/FY2015–16 testing; causal effect of term divisibility; final approval-time predictor availability; LGD or expected loss from gross charged-off amount divided by approval amount; production readiness.

Prediction artifact caveat: the malformed FY2024 loan-level prediction archive was marked invalid and excluded from curated release. Use aggregate metrics and verified frozen artifacts only; do not recover or publish individual predictions.

Outstanding scientific checks: feature versions at origination, strict-cohort selection bias, incomplete source-to-model reproduction, rare-outcome calibration, reporting uncertainty, independent review and code licensing.

Useful safe commands: see REPRODUCE.md for synthetic test commands, report checksum verification and release validation. Do not refit/rescore models solely to update documentation.
