The technical idea: Credit Risk Model Validation & Decisioning Workbench

What makes it stand out: most beginner portfolios stop at “train a classifier and report accuracy.” Your project will show the full model-risk lifecycle: scorecard baseline, challenger model, probability calibration, decision-threshold economics, subgroup diagnostics, explainability, drift tests, software tests and a documented validation opinion.

Dataset: UCI Default of Credit Card Clients — 30,000 rows and 23 features, CC BY 4.0. It is a historical Taiwan consumer-credit dataset from 2005. Cite it correctly and explicitly state that it is an educational benchmark, not current bank data or a proxy for banking-sector portfolios.
